Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2025

🛡️

TL;DR: Pdflex processes all files locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your files or personal data. Your privacy is our top priority.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pdflex ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application Pdflex — PDF Editor & Scanner ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT do:

  • We do not collect your personal information
  • We do not access your files beyond the documents you select
  • We do not upload your files to any server
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not require account creation or login

3. File Processing

All file processing — including PDF conversion, merging, splitting, scanning, and image extraction — happens entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to external servers. Temporary files created during processing are stored in your device's cache and are automatically cleaned up by the operating system.

4. Device Permissions

Our App may request the following permissions:

  • Photo Library: To select images for PDF conversion
  • Camera: To capture photos and scan documents for PDF conversion
  • File Access: To select and save PDF documents

These permissions are only used for the specific features you initiate. We never access your photos, camera, or files without your explicit action.

5. Advertising

Our App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use certain device data for ad serving, including:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • App usage data (ad interaction events)

We use non-personalized ads by default. You can manage your ad preferences and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements
  • Expo/EAS: For app updates and build distribution

7. Data Security

Since all processing occurs locally on your device, your files are protected by your device's built-in security features including encryption and biometric locks. We do not store any data on external servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Deny or revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Delete all locally stored files created by the App
  • Request information about data processing

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
