Privacy Policy

Pdflex — PDF Editor & Scanner

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pdflex ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Data Collection

Pdflex is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All PDF files and documents you create or edit remain on your device.

Advertising

Pdflex uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain data for the purpose of serving ads, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address (anonymized)
  • General location (country/region level)
  • App usage data (ad interactions)

We use non-personalized ads by default, which means ads are not based on your personal interests or browsing history. For more information about Google's data practices, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

File Access

Pdflex requires access to your device's photo library and camera to provide scanning and image-to-PDF conversion features. These permissions are used solely for the intended functionality:

  • Photo Library: To select images for PDF conversion
  • Camera: To scan documents

Images and files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Data Storage

All files created by Pdflex are stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud storage, external servers, or any remote databases. You have full control over your files at all times.

Third-Party Services

Pdflex uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (Privacy Policy)

Children's Privacy

Pdflex is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
