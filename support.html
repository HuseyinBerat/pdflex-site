Troubleshooting

Quick Fixes

🔄

Restart the App

Swipe up to close Pdflex completely, then reopen. This resolves most temporary issues.

📲

Update iOS

Go to Settings → General → Software Update. Make sure you're running the latest iOS version.

🗑️

Reinstall App

Delete Pdflex and reinstall from the App Store. This ensures you have the latest version.

💾

Free Up Storage

Large PDF operations need free space. Check Settings → General → iPhone Storage.
