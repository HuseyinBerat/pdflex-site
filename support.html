Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert images to PDF?

Open Pdflex, tap "Image to PDF" from the home screen, select your images from the photo library, arrange them in your preferred order, and tap the convert button.

How do I merge multiple PDFs?

Tap "Merge PDF" on the home screen, select the PDF files you want to combine, arrange their order, and tap merge. Your combined PDF will be saved automatically.

Is my data safe?

Yes! All files are processed and stored locally on your device. We never upload your documents to any server. Your privacy is our priority.

How do I scan a document?

Tap "Scan Document" on the home screen, point your camera at the document, and the app will automatically detect the edges. Tap capture to scan and save as PDF.

Can I password-protect my PDFs?

Yes! Use the "Protect PDF" tool to add password encryption to any PDF file. You can also remove passwords using the "Unlock PDF" tool.

How do I switch between Dark and Light mode?

Go to Settings tab, find the "Appearance" section, and choose between Dark, Light, or System theme.
